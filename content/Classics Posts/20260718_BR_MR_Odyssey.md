This is my review of both the book: **The Odyssey** by Homer (Greek poet) and the movie: **The Odyssey** directed by Christopher Nolan (British-American film maker). 

### The Odyssey (Book)

I decided to read **The Odyssey** by *Homer* (Greek poet) end-to-end as a personal challenge before the Christopher Nolan movie came out on Fri. 7/17/26. I was thankfully able to finish reading the book on the same day that the movie came out after almost a month of intensive focus. Under normal circumstances, I think it would have been extremely difficult for me to muster the motivation and energy to read a book such as The Odyssey, a book that’s been known to be quite challenging to read. I wanted to challenge myself because I had never read The Odyssey before in high school, so I viewed the movie release as a nice opportunity to immerse myself in Odysseus’ 20-year hero’s journey. I have no background in classics, but I've become an amateur classicist and find that I enjoy reading and learning about the ancient world quite a lot. I also didn’t want to pay for a copy of The Odyssey, so I found a translation of the book online for free by Butcher and Lang that was written in an archaic style of prose. Boy was it difficult to understand the English! But somehow I did it and made it through the book end-to-end and this was a second challenge that I overcame. Though The Odyssey is a fictional book in the form of an epic poem by Homer, it was a really interesting book once you got into it and actually started understanding the plot.
