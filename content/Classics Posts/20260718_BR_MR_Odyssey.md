I finished reading The **Odyssey** by Homer (Greek poet) and watched The Odyssey by Christopher Nolan on Fri. 7/17/26! I finished reading the book on the same day that the movie came out. I will write a detailed review of both because I've learned a lot by immersing myself in The Odyssey!

### The Odyssey (Book)

I decided to read The Odyssey by Homer end-to-end as a challenge before the Chris Nolan movie came out. Under normal circumstances, I think it would have been extremely difficult for me to muster the motivation and energy to read a book such as The Odyssey end-to-end, but the movie release acted as a very good timeboxed deadline for me. I wanted to challenge myself because I had never read The Odyssey before in high school, so I viewed the movie release as a nice opportunity to immerse myself in Odysseus’ 20-year hero’s journey. I have no background in classics, but I've become an amateur classicist and find that I enjoy reading and learning about the ancient world quite a lot. Though The Odyssey is a fictional book in the form of an epic poem by Homer (Greek poet), it was a really interesting book once you got into it and actually started understanding the plot.
